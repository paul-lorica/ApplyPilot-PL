## Notes

- "Top" here means prominent and strategically relevant in payments technology, not a strict ranking by market cap, revenue, or private valuation.
- Job-board URLs are company-owned careers pages where available. Some companies route their listings through ATS providers such as Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Eightfold, or SmartRecruiters after the careers landing page.
- Regional companies are included because payments is highly local: India, Brazil, Latin America, Europe, and Africa each have major payment networks and processors that are highly relevant to product and engineering roles.
